TrueProfit — Privacy Policy

App: TrueProfit (package com.trueprofit.app) · Developer: Aadi Labs · Effective date: 20 November 2026

TrueProfit is a profit tracker for gig drivers. It helps you record your trips and expenses and shows your real profit after mileage and vehicle costs. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

Your data stays on your phone. We do not run servers that collect your trips, expenses, or earnings. We do not sell or share your personal data, and the app has no account or sign-up.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter in TrueProfit — trips, expenses, vehicles, goals, settings, and photos of receipts you attach — is stored only in the app's private storage on your own device. The app works fully offline. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted with it. You can also erase all of it at any time from the app's Settings.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect Google Drive in the app, TrueProfit saves a backup copy of your data in your own Google Drive, in a special app-data folder that only TrueProfit can access. This is your backup, in your account: we cannot see it, read it, or use it. If you never connect Drive, nothing is uploaded anywhere. You can disconnect Drive and delete the backup from the app at any time.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

TrueProfit is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, in line with Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's policies. You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). We do not receive your advertising identifier ourselves.

No tracking by us

Aadi Labs does not include any analytics or tracking tools of its own in TrueProfit. We do not track how you use the app, and we do not collect your name, email address, location, or contacts through the app.

Children

TrueProfit is a work tool for drivers and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
